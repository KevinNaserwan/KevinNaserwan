### 👨‍💻 About Me

Experienced **Application Developer** and **Distributed Systems / AI Engineer** with deep expertise in enterprise microservices, high-throughput backend pipelines, and applied geospatial intelligence. Alumnus of **Universitas Sriwijaya (Informatics Engineering)** and former Core Team member at **Google Developer Student Club (GDSC) UNSRI**.

- 🏢 **Current Role**: **Application Developer** at **PT Bukit Asam (Persero) Tbk** (Enterprise Microservices, NestJS, Scalable Systems).
- 💼 **Industry Background**:
  - **HashMicro**: Fullstack Developer (Core Web Vitals optimization across 4,100+ URLs, Go & NestJS microservices, AI API automation with Gemini/Claude/OpenAI, Docker/Kubernetes & Grafana).
  - **Meets Indonesia**: Founder & Backend Developer (Go, NestJS, RabbitMQ, Redis, Flutter).
  - **R17 Group**: Full Stack Engineer (Enterprise SIAKAD for Universitas Padjadjaran via NestJS & Next.js).
  - **Serenade Story**: Backend Developer (High-volume e-commerce, WebSockets real-time scoring, Elasticsearch).
  - **Suitmedia Digital Agency** & **PT Pos Indonesia (Persero)**: Enterprise Web & Backend Engineering.
- ⚽ **Competitive Focus**: Participant in **More Than a Hack 2027** organized by **Mobile World Capital Barcelona, Barça Innovation Hub & Metrica Sports** (Focus: Tracking Homography & Pitch Possession Estimation).
- 🎓 **M.Sc Research Track**: Spatio-Temporal AI & Satellite Remote Sensing for **National Food Security (Rice Yield Forecasting)** and **Peatland Wildfire (*Karhutla*) Early Warning Systems** in Indonesia.
