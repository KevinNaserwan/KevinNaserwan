### 👨‍💻 About Me

Experienced **Software Engineer** and **AI / Geospatial Researcher** with a strong background in distributed backend architectures, scalable systems, and applied machine learning. Alumnus of **Universitas Sriwijaya (Informatics Engineering)** and former Core Team member at **Google Developer Student Club (GDSC) UNSRI**.

- 🔭 **Current Industry Roles**: Software Engineering across enterprise ERP systems, microservices, and high-frequency backend services.
- ⚽ **Active Challenge**: Competing in **More Than a Hack 2027** organized by **Mobile World Capital Barcelona, Barça Innovation Hub & Metrica Sports** (Focus: Planar Homography, Pitch Control & Tracking Possession).
- 🎓 **Research / M.Sc Track**: Applied Spatio-Temporal AI & Remote Sensing for **National Food Security (Crop Yield Forecasting)** and **Peatland Wildfire (*Karhutla*) Early Warning Systems** in Indonesia.
- 💬 **Ask Me About**: `.NET / C#`, `Python`, `Go`, `TypeScript`, `Clean Architecture & DDD`, `Computer Vision & Spatial Analytics`, `Earth Observation / GEE`.
